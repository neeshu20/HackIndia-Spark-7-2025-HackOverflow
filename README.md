🌱 GreenTrust: A Decentralized Renewable Energy Marketplace with AI-Powered Energy Insights
________________________________________
📌 What is it?
GreenTrust is a platform where:
•	People who produce renewable energy (like from solar panels)
•	Can sell the extra energy to others nearby
At the same time,
•	An AI system gives smart tips and forecasts about energy usage, savings, and efficiency.
________________________________________
💡 Simple Example:
Imagine you have solar panels at your home. On some days, you produce more energy than you need.
Instead of wasting it, you sell it on GreenTrust.
Now, your neighbor who doesn’t have solar panels can buy that clean energy from you — directly!
You both use the app, and the payment happens automatically through a smart contract (a safe auto-agreement on the blockchain).
________________________________________
🔍 What does the AI do?
The AI helps by:
•	Telling you how much energy you’ll likely produce tomorrow (based on weather).
•	Suggesting when to sell your energy to get the best value.
•	Giving personal tips on how to save more energy at home.
________________________________________
🧠 Why is this cool?
•	✅ No need for middlemen or big companies.
•	✅ All deals are secure and open, thanks to blockchain.
•	✅ AI makes everything smarter and more efficient.
•	✅ Supports clean energy and helps the planet!
